# 🚀 RevenueGuard AI

### AI-Powered Payment Failure Detection & Revenue Recovery Platform

RevenueGuard AI is an intelligent revenue recovery platform designed to help businesses identify failed payments, detect revenue at risk, understand payment failure causes, and recommend appropriate recovery actions through a centralized dashboard.

The platform combines payment monitoring, AI-based risk classification, root cause analysis, recovery decision-making, analytics, audit tracking, and an AI assistant into a single revenue recovery system.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Payment failures can result in significant revenue loss when they are not identified and addressed quickly.

Businesses need to know:

- Which payments have failed?
- How much revenue is currently at risk?
- Which failed payments require immediate attention?
- Why did the payment fail?
- What recovery action should be taken?
- How can recovery activities be monitored and audited?

RevenueGuard AI provides a centralized platform to analyze failed payments, prioritize risks, and recommend suitable recovery actions.
